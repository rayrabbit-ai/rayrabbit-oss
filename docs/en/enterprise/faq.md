# Dual Licensing Legal FAQ

Understand how RayRabbit's dual-licensing model works under the Open Source **AGPL v3** and our **Commercial Enterprise License** to ensure absolute compliance for your corporate applications.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Why does RayRabbit use a Dual Licensing model?
RayRabbit is committed to promoting standard open-source collaboration while offering commercial-grade, proprietary exemptions for enterprise developers.
* **AGPL v3 Core:** Keeps our standard protocol and bridges open-source, ensuring that enhancements made by the community return to the community.
* **Commercial Licenses:** Enable companies to integrate RayRabbit into their proprietary closed-source applications, platforms, or services without being bound by copyleft restrictions, while funding ongoing platform development.

---

### 2. What is the AGPL v3 license copyleft restriction?
The GNU Affero General Public License (AGPL v3) is a strong copyleft license. 
* If you modify RayRabbit or build proprietary agentic platforms that link directly with our AGPL-licensed core code, and run them over a network (e.g., serving users over HTTP or API), **you must make your entire agent application source code available to your users under the AGPL v3**.
* This is often highly undesirable for corporations building proprietary, competitive, or compliant internal workflows.

---

### 3. When do I need to acquire a Commercial License?
You should purchase a commercial Developer, Team, or Enterprise license if:
1. **Proprietary Integration:** You are building commercial software or closed-source products that embed or bundle RayRabbit core, and you cannot disclose your proprietary agent source code.
2. **Enterprise Security:** You require advanced enterprise-only modules such as **MAESTRO active isolated sandboxing**, enterprise SIEM aggregates, mTLS gateways, or enterprise KMS HSM automation.
3. **Professional SLA:** You need guaranteed incident response SLAs (< 2 hours) and dedicated support channels for production environments.

---

### 4. Can I use the AGPL v3 version inside my company for internal tools?
Yes, you can use the AGPL v3 version internally under the following conditions:
* If the tool is used exclusively by your internal staff, and you make no modifications that link proprietary code to the core in a way that triggers copyleft distribution requirements to external users.
* However, if the internal system interacts with external partners, clients, or external SaaS networks, it may trigger the "network interaction" copyleft clause of AGPL v3, requiring source code release. To eliminate all legal ambiguity, acquisition of a Commercial License is highly recommended.

---

### 5. Does RayRabbit offer indemnification under the Commercial License?
Yes. Our Enterprise Suite tier includes full **Intellectual Property (IP) Indemnification**, giving your compliance and legal teams absolute peace of mind against any third-party copyright claims. The standard AGPL v3 open-source core does not include any warranty or indemnification.
